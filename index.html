What is Critical Thinking about Performance?

Performance engineering isn't just about metrics and tools—it's about understanding the fundamental principles that govern how systems behave under load. Critical thinking allows us to question assumptions, validate hypotheses, and make informed decisions based on evidence rather than intuition alone.

Through our YouTube channel and community engagement, we share insights, case studies, and practical techniques to help you become a better performance professional.
